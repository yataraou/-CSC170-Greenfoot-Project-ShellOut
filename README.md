# [CSC170] Greenfoot Project: Shell Out!
Shell Out! is an arcade-style obstacle-avoidance game inspired by Frogger. Players control a small cartoon-style turtle attempting to navigate across a dangerous road to reach the safety of its home. Along the way, the turtle must avoid moving vehicles and carefully time its movements to survive. In addition to reaching home safely, the turtle must rescue its three missing children, represented by smaller green turtles who are scattered across the road, before a hungry flock of ravenous birds catches up to them. Players must successfully collect all three baby turtles and make it home safely within sixty seconds to win the game. Players earn points for rescuing their children and successfully reaching home, with additional points to be gained for speed of completion. Collisions with vehicles result in lost lives, and running out of lives before completing the rescue results in a game over. A sixty-second countdown timer adds another challenge, and running out of time before completing the rescue also results in a game over as the birds steal the remaining baby turtles.
